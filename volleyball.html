🏐 Court Dimensions & Equipment

Court Specifications

Court Size

18 meters (59 feet) long
9 meters (29.5 feet) wide

Free Zone

Minimum 2 meters (6.5 feet) surrounding the court
Recommended: 3 meters (10 feet)

Net Height

2.24 meters (7 feet, 4⅛ inches) at center

Attack Line

3 meters (9 feet, 10 inches) from center line

Court Markings

  • All lines: 5 cm (2 inches) wide, contrasting color
  • Boundary lines: Solid, uninterrupted sidelines and end lines
  • Center line: Divides court into two equal 9m x 9m courts
  • Service zone: 9-meter wide area behind end line, minimum 2-meter depth

The Ball

  • Circumference: 65-67 cm (25.6-26.4 inches)
  • Weight: 260-280 grams (9-10 ounces)
  • Pressure: 0.3-0.325 kg/cm² (4.3-4.6 psi)
  • Color: Uniform and light in color; at least one-third must be white or light
  • All balls in a match must be identical

📊 Scoring & Match Duration

Rally Scoring

A team scores a point when:

  • The ball contacts the opponent's court
  • The opposing team commits a fault
  • The opposing team receives a penalty

Winning a Set

Sets 1-4

First to 25 points with at least a 2-point lead

No point cap

Set 5 (Deciding Set)

First to 15 points with at least a 2-point lead

Teams switch sides at 8 points

Match Format

Best of 5 sets - First team to win 3 sets wins the match

Interval between sets: Maximum 3 minutes

Optional intermission: Between sets 2 and 3, up to 5 minutes (must include 3 minutes warmup)

👥 Teams & Players

Team Composition

  • 6 players on court - 3 front row, 3 back row
  • Unlimited substitutes on bench
  • Up to 2 liberos can be designated per set
  • Playing captain must be designated

Player Positions & Rotation

Position Numbers

  1. Right Back (Server)
  2. Right Front
  3. Center Front
  4. Left Front
  5. Left Back
  6. Center Back

Rotation Rules

When receiving team wins rally:

  • Team scores a point
  • Gains right to serve
  • Players rotate one position clockwise

Uniforms

Jersey Requirements:

  • Teammates must wear same color, style, cut, and trim (except libero)
  • Front number: Minimum 10 cm (4 inches) high
  • Back number: Minimum 15 cm (6 inches) high
  • Numbers 0-99 allowed (no duplicates)
  • Numbers must contrast with jersey color

Libero Uniform:

Must clearly contrast from teammates' predominant jersey color(s)

Jewelry & Equipment

  • Allowed: Small, snug-fitting jewelry (studs, posts, nose rings, ear cuffs) above the chin
  • Not allowed: Any jewelry below the chin, large hoops, dangly earrings, bracelets
  • Taping over jewelry is not permitted
  • Religious medallions/medical IDs must be removed from chains and taped/sewn under uniform

🏐 Playing the Ball

Team Hits

Maximum 3 successive hits per team to return ball (excluding blocks)

A player may not contact the ball twice in succession (except during blocking)

Legal Contact

  • Ball may contact any part of the body
  • Ball must be hit cleanly - not caught, held, or thrown
  • Ball can rebound in any direction
  • Simultaneous contacts by teammates count as one hit

Successive Contacts

✓ Legal

  • During blocking
  • Team's first hit
  • Team's second hit (if next contacted by teammate)

✗ Illegal

  • Two separate motions
  • Third team hit
  • Prolonged contact

Attack-Hit

Definition: Action directing ball toward opponent (not serve or block)

Back-Row Attack Restrictions:

Back-row player may NOT complete attack-hit when:

  • Player is in front zone
  • Ball is entirely above net at contact

Legal if: Player takes off behind attack line, OR ball is below net top at contact

Blocking

  • Only front-row players may complete a block
  • Block contact does NOT count as team hit
  • Team has 3 hits after a block
  • Blocker may make first hit after their own block
  • Back-row players and libero cannot block

Net Play

Net Contact:

Contact with net is a fault ONLY if:

  • Made during action of playing the ball, OR
  • It interferes with play

Note: Contact with hair is not a net fault

Center Line:

  • Crossing with foot/hand is OK if part remains on/above center line
  • Complete crossing is allowed if no interference

🎯 Service Rules

Service Execution

  1. Server must be in service zone (behind end line)
  2. Ball must be clearly released or tossed from hand(s)
  3. Must contact ball within 8 seconds of referee's authorization
  4. Only one toss allowed (considered part of service action)
  5. Hit with one hand or arm
  6. May not touch court or playing area during service

Service Faults

Serving team loses rally when:

  • Ball fails to cross net completely within antennas
  • Ball passes under net
  • Ball touches teammate or object before entering opponent's court
  • Ball lands out of bounds
  • Server touches court/end line during service
  • Server fails to contact ball within 8 seconds
  • Wrong server serves (rotation fault)

Screening

Serving team players must NOT prevent receiving team from seeing:

  • Contact of the serve
  • Path of the ball

Screening factors: player positions, path/speed/trajectory of serve

🛡️ The Libero Player

Libero Basics

  • Up to 2 liberos may be designated per set
  • Back-row specialist - restricted to back row only
  • Must wear contrasting uniform from teammates
  • Can replace any back-row player
  • Replacements are unlimited and don't count as substitutions

Libero Restrictions

Action Allowed? Details
Attack-hit ❌ NO Cannot attack when ball is entirely above net (anywhere on court)
Blocking ❌ NO Cannot block or attempt to block
Serving ✅ YES May serve in ONE rotation only per set
Setting from front zone ⚠️ LIMITED Teammate cannot attack overhand set when ball entirely above net

Libero Replacement Rules

  • Must enter/exit over sideline between attack line and end line
  • Can only replace back-row players
  • Must sit out at least one completed rally between replacements
  • Only one libero on court at a time
  • Two liberos may exchange freely when ball is out of play

🔄 Substitutions & Timeouts

Substitution Rules

Team Limits

  • 15 substitutions per set
  • Unlimited entries per player
  • Must return to original position in rotation

Procedures

  • Request when ball is out of play
  • Before authorization for service
  • Enter in substitution zone
  • Wait for referee authorization

Exceptional Substitution

When regular substitution not possible due to injury:

  1. Use player who played that position, OR
  2. Any substitute who hasn't played yet, OR
  3. Any substitute on bench, OR
  4. Libero (must wear regular jersey with legal number)

Exceptional substitutions don't count toward 15-substitution limit

Timeouts

  • 2 timeouts per team per set
  • 75 seconds duration (default)
  • Can be reduced to 60 seconds or increased to 90 seconds by mutual agreement
  • May be requested by coach or playing captain
  • Consecutive timeouts allowed

Media Timeouts:

For broadcast matches, automatic timeout when first team reaches 15 points (8 in deciding set)

⚠️ Sanctions & Misconduct

Individual Sanctions

Sanction Card Reason Consequence
Warning 🟨 Yellow Minor unsporting behavior No point penalty (first offense)
Penalty 🟥 Red Rude conduct, second minor offense Loss of rally, point to opponent
Disqualification 🟥🟨 Red + Yellow Aggression, extremely offensive conduct Loss of rally + point + expelled from match + next match

Minor Misconduct Examples

  • Inappropriately addressing opponents, spectators, or officials
  • Shouting and disrupting conduct of set
  • Repeatedly addressing officials about decisions
  • Displaying frustration

Rude Conduct Examples

  • Profanity
  • Deliberate interference with opponent
  • Demonstrative unsporting behavior
  • Illegal use of electronic aids

Delay Sanctions

First delay in set: 🟨 Warning

Subsequent delays: 🟥 Penalty (point to opponent)

Delay examples:

  • Failure to submit lineup on time
  • Substitution delay
  • Delay in returning from timeout
  • Second improper request in a set
  • Illegal jewelry/equipment causing delay

Improper Requests

First improper request: Denied, noted on scoresheet (no sanction)

Additional improper requests: Result in delay sanction

Examples:

  • Request after authorization for service
  • Request by non-authorized person
  • Third timeout request in a set
  • 16th substitution request

📋 Key Match Protocols

Prematch Warmup

Standard Protocol (60 minutes before match):

  • 41 minutes: Shared court warmup
  • 4 minutes: Visiting team exclusive
  • 4 minutes: Home team exclusive
  • 5 minutes: Visiting team exclusive
  • 5 minutes: Home team exclusive
  • 1 minute: Team huddle/announcements

Lineups due: 3 minutes before end of warmup (may change until 1 minute mark)

Challenge Review System (CRS)

If facility has video review capability:

  • Each team starts with 2 challenges per match
  • Additional challenge in 5th set (max 2 total)
  • Only head coach may request challenge
  • Must request immediately after play, before next service
  • Successful challenge is retained

Reviewable Decisions:

  • Ball in/out
  • Ball contact (touch)
  • Net faults
  • Service line faults
  • Back-row attack faults
  • Interference above the net

🎓 Quick Reference Summary

🏐 Basic Rules

  • 6 players per side
  • Best of 5 sets
  • 25 points (sets 1-4)
  • 15 points (set 5)
  • Rally scoring
  • 3 hits per side

⏱️ Time Limits

  • 8 seconds to serve
  • 75-second timeouts
  • 3-minute intervals
  • 2 timeouts/set
  • 15 substitutions/set

🛡️ Libero

  • Up to 2 per set
  • Back row only
  • Contrasting uniform
  • No attacking/blocking
  • Can serve (1 rotation)
  • Unlimited replacements

⚠️ Common Faults

  • Four hits
  • Double contact
  • Net fault
  • Back-row attack
  • Out of rotation
  • Foot fault on serve
