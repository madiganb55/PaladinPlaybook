Key Definitions

Base: A person in direct contact with the performing surface who supports another person's weight.
Top Person: A person being supported by another while off the performing surface or tossed into the air.
Spotter: A person responsible for assisting or catching the top person in a partner stunt or pyramid. Must focus on the head, neck, and shoulders of the top person and cannot hold objects.
Inversion: A body position where the shoulders are below the waist.
Cradle: A dismount where the top person lands in a face-up, semi-piked position.
Pyramid: A skill where a top person is supported by a middle layer person.
Rewind: A skill where the top person starts with at least one foot on the ground, is tossed into the air, and performs a flip into a stunt, pyramid, or cradle.

General Safety Guidelines

⚠️ Essential Safety Requirements

  • All squads must be under the direction of a qualified coach
  • All practice sessions must be supervised
  • Technical skills must NOT be performed on concrete, asphalt, wet, or uneven surfaces
  • Proper warm-up exercises must precede all activities
  • Mandatory training in proper spotting techniques
  • Skills not mastered should only be performed in supervised practice

Personal Safety Equipment

Prohibited: All jewelry (navel, tongue, earrings, necklaces). Medical bracelets allowed if taped.
Required: Soft-soled athletic shoes. Gymnastics shoes, jazz shoes, and boots are prohibited.
Nails: Must be kept short, near the end of the fingers (including artificial nails).

Partner Stunts

When Spotters Are Required:

  • Released load-ins and transitions with more than 1¼ twists
  • Dismounts with more than 1¼ twists (spotter must assist on cradle)
  • One-arm stunts (except basic cupie/awesome or liberty)
  • Single base release to shoulders
  • Extended handstand positions
  • Releases from handstand position on ground

Rotation Limits:

Twisting mounts and dismounts greater than 2¼ rotations are prohibited.

Flipping Rules:

Flipping into and out of stunts is allowed with restrictions:
  • Top person limited to 1¼ flipping rotations and one twisting rotation
  • Must be caught by original group
  • Bases limited to ½ turn under the top person
  • Rewinds require a spotter
  • Flips from stunts must initiate from prep level or below
  • Flips to vertical stunts require a spotter
  • Flips with one rotation or less require two people
  • Flips with more than one rotation require three people

Prohibited Skills:

  • Single based split catches
  • Front, back and side tension drops
  • Most leg pitch, toe pitch, walk-in, sponge, and straddle catch flips to performing surface

Pyramids

Height Limit: Pyramids higher than 2½ body lengths are prohibited.
  • Chairs, thigh stands, shoulder straddles = 1½ body lengths
  • Shoulder stands = 2 body lengths
  • Extended stunts = 2½ body lengths

Spotter Requirements:

  • At least two spotters for each person above two persons high
  • One spotter behind, one in front or at the side
  • Spotters must be in position as the top person lands in the 2½ high position
  • Cannot place hands directly under the sole of the foot of a middle layer
  • Front/side spotter may place one hand under foot or hands of base

Dismounts from Pyramids:

Flipping dismounts allowed:
  • Front flip and Arabian may land in cradle, horizontal position, or performing surface
  • Front flip with up to one complete twist may land in two-person cradle only
  • Must use at least two catchers, one on each side

Basket Tosses

Basic Requirements:
  • Must be performed from ground level only
  • Maximum of four bases
  • Must be cradled by three of the original bases
  • One catcher must catch in a scoop under the head and shoulders
  • Top person cannot hold anything except poms

Non-Flipping Baskets:

Limited to 2¼ twisting rotations and three total tricks (e.g., kick double full).

Flipping Baskets - Allowed Skills:

  • Non-twisting backward somersaults (tuck, layout, or pike) with up to one additional non-flipping trick
  • Non-twisting forward somersaults (arched, tuck, layout, or pike) with no additional tricks
  • Back Layout Full Twist
  • Special skills requiring all male side throwers and backspot: Layout Straddle Full, Tuck X Out Full, Layout Kick Full, Layout Double Full
Prohibited: Front Fulls, Arabians, Pike-open Full Twist, Side Sumis, Kick Double Full Twists, Double Flips and greater.

Tumbling

Prohibited:
  • Tumbling over, under, or through partner stunts, pyramids, or individuals
  • Skills exceeding one flipping rotation
  • Skills with two or more twisting rotations
  • Dive rolls
  • Airborne drops to prone position (e.g., back flip to pushup position)

Surface Restrictions

Skills Requiring Mat, Grass, or Rubberized Track:

  • Basket tosses and similar multi-based tosses
  • Inversion releases from ground or a skill
  • Flipping skills into or from stunts, tosses, or pyramids
  • 2½ high pyramids

Basketball/Volleyball/Indoor Games:

Prohibited During Timeouts (Regardless of Matting):

  • Basket tosses and similar tosses
  • One-arm partner stunts (except non-twisting cupies/awesomes)
  • Flips into or from partner stunts
  • Full releases to/from inversions
  • Extended inversions
  • Twisting dismounts greater than 1¼ rotation
  • 2½ high pyramids
  • Airborne twisting tumbling skills

NCA Competition Rules

Performance Area

Competition Surface

  • 42' deep x 54' wide carpeted mat
  • Spring floors are prohibited for school-based programs
  • Props may be placed outside the competition area but cannot be thrown

Routine Requirements

Time and Team Size:
  • Maximum time: 2 minutes 15 seconds (135 seconds)
  • Team size: 10-20 participants (plus one mascot)
  • 5 point deduction per person over/under limits
  • Mascot may NOT participate in technical skills or spot
Performance Guidelines:
  • Music allowed for all or part of routine
  • No organized formal entrances with stunts/tumbling
  • Timing begins with first note of music or movement
  • All performers must have at least one foot on surface when routine starts
  • Allowed props: flags, banners, megaphones, pom pons, signs

Collegiate Image & Uniform Requirements

Female Uniform Standards:

  • No "bra top" style - material must fall 1 inch below bra line
  • No extremely low-cut necklines or excessively bare/backless tops
  • Must have straps over shoulder or around neck (no tube tops)
  • Must cover briefs when feet shoulder-width apart
  • Skirts must fall 1.5 inches below boy-short briefs or 2 inches below standard briefs
  • No ultra-low-rise waistbands (no lower than 2 inches below navel)

Male Uniform Standards:

  • Full torso length covering shoulders to waist
  • Short or long sleeves covering at least midway between shoulder and elbow
  • Pants must cover waist to feet
  • Shorts must fall no higher than mid-thigh
Hair Bow Policy: Hair bows may only be worn behind the top center of the head and must be small to medium in size. Violations in finals can result in a 1.0 Collegiate Image deduction.

1-4 male participants

All Girl:

No male participants

Intermediate Division Restrictions

⚠️ Intermediate Level Restrictions

Stunts/Pyramids:
  • Release inversions allowed but may not exceed prep level or ½ twist
  • Free flipping mounts/dismounts not allowed
  • Release full twists to extended position prohibited
  • Twisting dismounts limited to 1¼ twist
  • Pyramids sustained over 2 persons high prohibited
  • Twisting stunts limited to 1¼ rotation (no 1½ ups or double ups)
Tosses:
  • Release flips from basket/sponge tosses prohibited
  • Twisting dismounts may not exceed 2 rotations
Tumbling:
  • Standing back tucks or other flips prohibited
  • Airborne flips out of running tumbling permitted (tucks, layouts, whips)
  • Twisting flips prohibited (includes aerials and onodis)

Deductions & Violations

Point Deduction System

Infraction Deduction
Athlete Fall (hand/knee down in tumbling) 0.25
Building Bobble (control mistakes, no fall) 0.25
Building Fall (drop to cradle/load position) 0.75
Major Building Fall (top person to surface) 1.25
Maximum per stunt/pyramid 1.75
Maximum for pyramid (total) 3.5

Safety & Rule Violations

  • Building skills out of level: 1.0 deduction
  • Tumbling skills out of level: 0.5 deduction
  • Boundary violation: 0.25 per occurrence (both feet or upper torso outside boundary)
  • Leaving performance surface: 1.0 penalty
  • Inappropriate choreography: 1.0 per violation

Time Violations

  • 1+ seconds over: 0.25 deduction
  • 6+ seconds over: 1.0 deduction

Note: 3-second buffer for human error. Deductions only assessed if time clearly exceeds limit.

Major Violations

  • Unsportsmanlike conduct: 2.0 deduction and potential removal/disqualification
  • Division violations (crossover): 5.0 deduction per illegal participant (max 10.0)
  • Team size violations: 5.0 deduction per person over/under limits

Music & Choreography

Deductions given for:
  • Swear words or connotations of sexual acts
  • Drug references
  • Mention of specific body parts
  • Violent acts or behavior
  • Vulgar or suggestive choreography (hip thrusting, inappropriate touching/positioning)

Eligibility & Qualification

Eligibility Requirements

Academic Requirements:

  • Must be full-time student or enrolled in minimum 9 credit hours
  • Graduating seniors/grad students may enroll in fewer hours if working to graduate
  • Must be current member of the team represented

Competition Limits:

  • May only represent ONE school in ONE National Championship per academic year
  • Maximum of FIVE (5) National Championships total (cheer, dance, JC, 4-year)
  • Junior College: Maximum THREE (3) Championships during college career
  • Coach must be currently USA Cheer certified

How to Qualify

Camp Qualification:

Attend NCA/NDA Collegiate Summer Camp with minimum 8 team members and earn a bid through participation, conduct, and camp achievements.

Video Qualification:

Submit video by January 19 showing:

  • Stunts: At least 3 complete stunts with dismounts
  • Tumbling: At least 2 standing skills and 2 synchronized running passes
  • Tosses: At least 3 complete tosses from mount through cradle
  • Pyramids: At least 3 complete pyramids from mount through dismount
  • Maximum 2 minutes total

Important: These rules are general in nature and not intended to cover all circumstances. All cheerleading skills should be carefully reviewed and supervised by a qualified coach.
