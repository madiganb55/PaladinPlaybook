Dual Match Format

Standard Format (Division I, II, III, NAIA)

7 Team Points Total:

  • Three doubles matches (worth 1 combined point)
  • Six singles matches (each worth 1 point)
  • First team to 4 points wins

Doubles Format

6-game set with no-ad scoring

7-point tiebreak at 6-all

No warm-up

Singles Format

Best of 3 sets with no-ad scoring

7-point tiebreak at 6-all

No warm-up

Shortened Format

Once match is decided:

Remaining matches in 2nd set play 10-point tiebreak for 3rd set

Already in 3rd set play to completion

🆕 Division III Change (2025-26)

Division III now uses the same 7-point dual match format with "play-play" as default (all doubles matches played to completion unless coaches agree otherwise).

Scoring System

No-Ad Scoring

All divisions use no-ad scoring for both singles and doubles:

  • At deuce (40-40), the next point wins the game
  • Receiver chooses which side to receive from
  • Once chosen, the receiver cannot change sides if the point is replayed

Service Lets

IMPORTANT: There are NO service lets in ITA tennis. If the serve hits the net and lands in the proper service box, the ball is in play.

Time Between Points

Activity Time Allowed
Between points 25 seconds (both server and receiver)
Changeover 90 seconds
Set break 120 seconds (2 minutes)
Between doubles and singles 5 minutes

Making Calls

Player Responsibilities

Players have primary responsibility for making line calls on their side of the net.

Benefit of Doubt

When in doubt, the call goes to the opponent. The ball is good unless you clearly see space between the ball and the line.

Immediate Calls

Out calls must be made immediately - before the opponent hits the return or before it goes out of play.

Unsighted Balls

There are NO "unsighted" calls. If you don't see the ball, call it good.

Correcting Errors

If you realize you made a mistake, reverse your call. The point goes to your opponent (never replayed).

Doubles Calls

Partner Disagreement: If doubles partners disagree on a call, the ball is good. The point goes to the opponents (never replayed).

Overrules by Officials

Number of Overrules Penalty
1st overrule Loss of point
2nd overrule Loss of point + Code Violation (Point Penalty)
3rd overrule Loss of point + Code Violation (Game Penalty)
4th overrule Loss of point + DEFAULT

Code of Conduct

Point Penalty System

Inappropriate conduct is penalized progressively:

Offense Penalty
1st Code Violation Point Penalty
2nd Code Violation Game Penalty
3rd Code Violation DEFAULT
NOTE: There is NO warning in the ITA Point Penalty System. The first offense results in a point penalty.

Violations Include:

  • Visible or audible obscenity/profanity
  • Racquet abuse
  • Ball abuse
  • Verbal or physical abuse of official or player
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct
  • Time delays
  • Being overruled excessively

Immediate Default Offenses

The following result in IMMEDIATE DEFAULT:

  • Hostile profanity directed at opponent, coach, official, or spectator
  • Ethnic, religious, racial, or homophobic slurs
  • Physical contact with opponent, coach, spectator, or official
  • Ball or racquet thrown in anger that strikes someone

Coaching Rules

Who Can Coach

  • Head coach and up to 2 other designated people
  • Designated coaches (associate, assistant, volunteer)
  • Designated Player Assistants (DPAs) - players not competing in that segment
  • Maximum of 3 coaches/DPAs at one time

When Coaching is Allowed

Coaching is only allowed when the ball is NOT in play

Coaches who interfere with play (making calls, giving instructions during points) cause their player to lose the point.

Where Coaches Can Coach

Coaches may coach from their team's end of the court. During points, coaches may:

  • Sit on player bench or stand at net post
  • Stand on their side between net post and service line
  • Stand on empty adjacent court on their side (no closer than doubles sideline)
  • Stand on empty adjacent court on opponent's side (no closer than mid-court line)

Electronic Devices

  • Only coaches may use devices for texting/data
  • NO voice features allowed
  • NO video replay or player analysis during match
  • Players cannot use electronic devices at all
  • Smartwatches allowed for health/wellness only

Timeouts and Breaks

Medical Timeout

One medical timeout per match (5 minutes max)

  • Player is assessed 1 point for taking a medical timeout
  • Treatment on changeovers/set breaks doesn't count as MTO
  • Player needing additional MTO must retire

Bathroom Breaks

When Time Allowed
One bathroom break per match Up to 5 minutes (at set break only)
During changeover/set break Within the 90 sec/2 min allowed
Other times Uses medical timeout

Concussion Timeout

Separate from medical timeout

  • 15 minutes max for evaluation
  • No point penalty assessed
  • Must be cleared by qualified medical personnel

Extreme Heat Rule

If temperature is 90°F or higher at match start, players are offered a 10-minute rest between 2nd and 3rd sets.

Key Lineup Rules

Order of Ability

Players MUST compete in order of ability:

  • Best player at #1, second best at #2, etc.
  • Players clearly stronger cannot be moved down
  • Players of equal ability may alternate adjacent positions

Lineup Changes

Between back-to-back matches: Players can move up only ONE position from previous match

During a match: Substitutions allowed within 2 minutes after doubles point is completed

Minimum Requirements

  • Four physically able players
  • One institutional coaching staff member

Quick Reference

Match Start

• Lineups exchanged 1 hour before

• No warm-up for doubles or singles

• 5 minutes between doubles and singles

Officials Required

• Division I: 2 certified officials required

• Other divisions: 1 recommended

• Must have current ITA certification

Spectator Areas

• Bench players must stay in designated "allowable area"

• Cheering must be positive

• No noise during points

Tennis Balls

• Official ball: Dunlop Grand Prix Extra Duty

• Division I: New balls each set

• Other divisions: Change after 2nd set
